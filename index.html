About us

Let us tell you our story

Across India, goods travel on handwritten consignment notes, phone-call prices and guesswork. Our founders saw this first-hand in a family cargo business, and decided small transporters and drivers deserve the same smart tools big companies have.

CargoMind AI makes freight simpler: less typing, fairer prices, reliable delivery times and early warnings, all in the language you are comfortable with.
